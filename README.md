<h1>Fretwise privacy policy</h1>
<p>Last updated: 14 October 2026</p>
<p>Fretwise is a fretboard training app. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.</p>
<p>Your settings, game scores and per-note practice statistics are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.</p>
<p>Fretwise has no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. It does not need an internet connection to work.</p>
<p>The app uses the vibration motor for answer feedback. It does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.</p>
